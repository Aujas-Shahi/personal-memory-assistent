PACKING NOTES FOR A THREE-DAY JOURNEY

CHECKLIST
---------
Drafted 9 October 2025 while planning a short trip from Pune to Nashik. This is a general list rather than a reservation. Keep travel essentials in a small day bag and less urgent items in the main backpack.
• Government photo ID and digital backup stored offline
• Ticket confirmation, hotel voucher and emergency contact card
• Charger, power bank, cable and wired earphones
• Refillable bottle, light snacks and prescribed personal essentials
• Comfortable shoes, one spare top, small towel and weather layer

PLANNING REMINDERS
------------------
Download maps before leaving because station basements and rural road sections may have weak mobile data. Tell a flatmate the approximate route and expected return time. If the trip involves an overnight train, keep a lock for the luggage and avoid leaving valuables near the aisle. Confirm the platform and departure time from the official railway display rather than relying solely on an old screenshot.
A flexible schedule is usually better than assigning every hour to an attraction. Identify one must-see place and one indoor alternative for bad weather. Keep some cash for small vendors, but do not carry the entire budget in one pocket. Share expenses in the group ledger the same evening to prevent forgotten payments.
