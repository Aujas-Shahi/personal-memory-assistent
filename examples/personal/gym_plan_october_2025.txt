FOUR-WEEK GYM AND MOBILITY PLAN
===============================
Fictional demo record | For semantic-search testing only

STARTING POINT
--------------
Plan drafted on 1 October 2025 for a fictional student who has access to a basic campus gym. Goal: build a repeatable routine, improve general strength and avoid spending every session on random machines. This is general fitness planning, not a medical or rehabilitation prescription. Start with manageable loads and ask the floor trainer for equipment guidance.

WEEKLY STRUCTURE
----------------
Monday - full body A: goblet squat 3 x 8; machine chest press 3 x 8-10; seated row 3 x 10; hip hinge practice 2 x 10; easy cycle 8 minutes. Tuesday - 20 to 30 minute brisk walk and mobility. Wednesday - full body B: leg press 3 x 10; lat pulldown 3 x 10; dumbbell overhead press 2 x 8; glute bridge 3 x 12; plank 3 short sets. Thursday - rest or a relaxed walk. Friday - repeat A or B based on soreness and time. Weekend - one outdoor walk and one rest day.
Warm up for five to eight minutes and perform lighter practice sets before working sets. Rest 60-120 seconds depending on the exercise. Use a range of motion that feels controlled, not painful. If a movement causes sharp pain, stop rather than trying to push through. Progress by adding a repetition or a small amount of weight only when technique remains consistent for all sets.

TRACKING AND RECOVERY
---------------------
Record exercise, load, repetitions and perceived effort in a simple log. The first two weeks should feel repeatable rather than exhausting. Aim for consistent sleep and regular meals; no supplement purchase is required to follow this plan. On exam weeks, reduce the plan to two short sessions rather than abandoning it entirely. Keep gym shoes, towel and water bottle ready the night before to reduce friction.
Example check-in on 28 October: attended 10 of 16 planned sessions, increased row from 15 kg to 20 kg, and missed two Friday sessions due to lab submissions.
